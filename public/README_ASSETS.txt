=== SISTER BIRTHDAY SURPRISE ASSETS GUIDE ===

To personalize this website with your real video, music, and photos:

1. VIDEO:
   - Place your video file in this directory:
     public/birthday-video.mp4
   - The video card and lightbox modal will automatically play this file!

2. BACKGROUND MUSIC:
   - Place your audio file in this directory:
     public/birthday-music.mp3
   - The floating music player in the bottom-right corner will play it when clicked.

3. PHOTOS / MEMORIES:
   - Place your 8 photos in the images directory:
     public/images/memory1.jpg
     public/images/memory2.jpg
     public/images/memory3.jpg
     public/images/memory4.jpg
     public/images/memory5.jpg
     public/images/memory6.jpg
     public/images/memory7.jpg
     public/images/memory8.jpg

4. CUSTOMIZING TEXT & DATES:
   - Edit "src/data/content.ts" to customize the dates, captions, and personal notes anytime!
